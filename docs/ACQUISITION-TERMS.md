# Acquisition Terms

These terms are intended for demo or acquisition discussions and are not legal advice.

## Summary

This project may be evaluated as a lightweight B2B productivity tool focused on LinkedIn prospecting workflows. It is structured as a static, low-cost SaaS concept with simple deployment and minimal technical overhead.

## Assumptions for evaluation

- low operating cost
- very small product surface
- user data stored locally unless future backend features are added
- strong fit for founder/operator productivity use cases

## Common evaluation considerations

- the value is in workflow simplification, not automation
- product differentiation comes from strong templates and search quality
- margins scale well because deployment remains static and light

## Notes

If the product were acquired or expanded, the next natural step would be to add an optional SaaS layer for cloud backup, multi-device sync, and team collaboration.
