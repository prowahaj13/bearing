# Product Overview

Bearing is a lightweight static web app for helping operators research and contact people on LinkedIn more deliberately. Instead of building a large CRM or complex backend, it keeps the experience focused on a few useful decisions:

- choose a goal (job search, hiring, clients, networking)
- pick a category and refine with keywords/location
- generate LinkedIn search links and a short outreach message
- track a lead list in-browser with statuses and priority scoring

## Core value proposition

The product reduces decision fatigue. Users do not need to craft a new search from scratch for every outreach workflow. Bearing structures the process around a repeatable search pattern and gives a first draft message immediately.

## Target user

The primary audience is a founder, operator, recruiter, or freelancer who wants to use LinkedIn intentionally without automation or scraping. 

## Product principles

1. Simple and privacy-first: all data stays in the browser.
2. Search-first workflow: people choose a goal, then generate target search links.
3. Human outreach: message templates are a starting point, not a replacement for personalization.
4. Static deployment: easy hosting and low operational burden.
