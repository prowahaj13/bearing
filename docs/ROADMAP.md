# Roadmap

## Phase 1: launch-ready MVP

- static landing/app experience
- category and goal-based LinkedIn search generation
- lead import, scoring, and status tracking
- local backup/restore support

## Phase 2: product polish

- richer categories and industry filters
- more outreach templates for different products
- CSV import improvements and deduplication options
- optional export templates for sales ops teams

## Phase 3: monetization and distribution

- branded static landing pages for different user segments
- template packs for recruiters, founders, and agencies
- documentation and instruction content for wider adoption

## Phase 4: advanced workflows

- optional sync layer or server-backed storage for teams
- analytics on lead quality and outreach conversion
- per-template customization and reusable outreach flows
